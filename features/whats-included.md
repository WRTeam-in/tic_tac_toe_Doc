# What's Included

## What You'll Getw

Your purchase includes all the essential resources required to customize, build, and publish your own branded version of the application.

- **Complete Flutter Source Code** — a fully functional Flutter project containing every screen, feature, and gameplay mode shown in the demo.
- **Firebase Integration** — pre-configured Firebase integration for authentication, multiplayer functionality, cloud storage, and player data management.
- **Installation Documentation** — comprehensive step-by-step docs covering project setup, Firebase configuration, platform-specific settings, and deployment (see [installation docs](/mobile-app/)).
- **6 Months Free Support** — professional technical support for installation, setup questions, and product-related issues during the included support period.
- **Lifetime Free Updates** — future product improvements, enhancements, compatibility updates, and bug fixes at no additional cost.

## Product Ecosystem

Every component works together to deliver a seamless player experience — from authentication and matchmaking to progression and monetization.

- **Player Authentication** — secure sign-in via Google, Apple, Email, or Guest Login with Firebase Authentication.
- **Multiplayer Engine** — Firebase Realtime Database powers online matchmaking and synchronizes gameplay between players in real time.
- **Gameplay Modes** — Online Multiplayer, Play Against AI, Pass & Play.
- **Player Progression** — Coin Collection, Leaderboards, Match History, Statistics, Cosmetic Skins.
- **Monetization** — Google Play In-App Purchases, Apple In-App Purchases, Google AdMob, Unity Ads.
- **Player Engagement** — Multiple Board Sizes, Mini Games, Multi-language Support, Profile Customization, Share App, Rate App, informational pages.
