## About Tic Tac Toe

**Tic Tac Toe – Complete Multiplayer Flutter Game Solution.** Launch your own fully branded Tic Tac Toe game with online multiplayer, AI gameplay, local multiplayer, in-app purchases, rewarded monetization, and Firebase-powered real-time matchmaking — all from a single Flutter codebase.

**Platforms:** Android · iOS · Flutter · Firebase
**Technologies:** Flutter • Firebase • AdMob • Unity Ads • Google Play Billing • Apple In-App Purchases

Build and publish your own engaging Tic Tac Toe experience without starting from scratch. This solution combines the timeless gameplay of Tic Tac Toe with modern multiplayer technology, player progression, and built-in monetization. Whether players want to challenge an AI opponent, compete with friends on the same device, or battle real players online, everything is already included.

## What This Product Solves

| Challenge | Solution |
|---|---|
| Building multiplayer games is time-consuming | Launch instantly with Firebase-powered real-time matchmaking |
| Monetization can be difficult to implement | Built-in In-App Purchases, AdMob, Unity Ads, and a permanent Remove Ads purchase |
| Players lose interest quickly | Keep users engaged with rankings, skins, coins, rewards, and multiple gameplay modes |
| Developing cross-platform apps doubles the work | One Flutter codebase for both Android and iOS |
| Backend development increases complexity | Firebase handles authentication, matchmaking, player data, and cloud storage |
