Gameplay Modes & Product Walkthrough

Reference for product walkthrough / demo videos. Each section below maps to one short video (30–60 seconds), showing real gameplay rather than static UI, with consistent branding, typography, and colors.

Getting Started​

Launch your Tic Tac Toe journey in minutes.

From creating an account to winning online matches, getting started is quick and simple: register, personalize a profile, and begin playing across multiple game modes.

Covers: Welcome Screen · Google Login · Apple Login · Email Registration · Guest Login · Free Welcome Coins · Home Screen Overview · Navigation · Profile Setup

Online Multiplayer​

Challenge real players around the world.

Competitive Tic Tac Toe with real-time online matchmaking. Select a wager, choose a board size, and get matched with another player instantly. Every match is synchronized through Firebase; winners earn double the coins.

Covers: Select Online Multiplayer · Choose Coin Entry · Choose Board Size · Matchmaking Screen · Finding Opponent · Live Gameplay · Winning Match · Coin Reward · Leaderboard Update · Match History

Play Against AI​

Practice anytime with an intelligent AI opponent.

Choose a board size and difficulty, then challenge Dora, the game's built-in AI — perfect for new players to learn and experienced players to practice strategy without risking coins.

Covers: Select AI Mode · Difficulty Selection · Board Selection · Gameplay · Win/Loss Screen

Pass & Play​

Enjoy local multiplayer with friends.

No internet needed — two players compete on the same device, choosing a board size and taking turns.

Covers: Start Pass & Play · Choose Board · Player Turns · Match Timer · Winner Announcement

Coin Store​

Expand gameplay with coins & premium purchases.

Players purchase additional coins through Google Play / Apple In-App Purchases to keep competing online.

Covers: Open Coin Store · Buy Coin Packages · Updated Coin Balance

Player Customization​

Personalize the gaming experience.

Unique username, profile picture, and collectible skins — unlock new skins with earned coins.

Covers: Edit Profile · Change Username · Upload Avatar · Browse Skins · Purchase Skin · Equip Skin

Rankings & Match History​

Track every victory.

Detailed statistics, global rankings, and complete online match history — wins, losses, coin transactions, leaderboard climb.

Covers: Leaderboard · Personal Ranking · Match Statistics · Win Count · Total Matches · Coin History

Mini Games​

More games, more entertainment.

Three classic arcade games included within the app for extra entertainment between competitive matches.

Covers: Open More Games · Hextris · Clumsy Bird · Pacman

Language & Settings​

A personalized experience for every player.

Switch languages, manage account, access important info, and personalize the app from one settings section.

Covers: Change Language · About Us · Contact Us · How to Play · Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions · Share App · Rate App · Logout · Delete Account

Firebase Setup (Optional Video)​

Configure Firebase & publish your game.

How to connect a Firebase project, configure authentication, enable multiplayer services, and prepare the app for Google Play and the App Store — see the installation docs for the written version of this flow.

Covers: Firebase Project Creation · Authentication Setup · Realtime Database · Storage · Google Sign-In · Apple Sign-In · Build & Run

Complete Player Journey​

Launch App → Login / Guest → Receive Welcome Coins → Home Screen → (AI Mode / Pass & Play / Online Multiplayer)

AI Mode:            Choose Board → Gameplay → Win/Loss Screen
Pass & Play:         Choose Board → Player Turns → Match Timer → Winner Announcement
Online Multiplayer:  Choose Coin Entry → Choose Board Size → Find Opponent → Live Gameplay
                     → Match Result (Win/Lose) → Coin Reward/Deduction → Leaderboard/Match History Updates
