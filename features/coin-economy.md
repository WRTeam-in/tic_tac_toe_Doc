# Competitive Coin Economy

**Every match has something at stake.**

<!-- TODO: replace with real coin wager / entry screen screenshot -->
![Coin economy placeholder](../static/images/placeholder.svg)

The integrated coin system transforms ordinary Tic Tac Toe into a competitive experience where players invest coins before every online match. Winners receive double the entry amount, while defeated players lose their wager, creating excitement in every game.

**Available entry levels:** 10 · 25 · 50 · 100 coins.
