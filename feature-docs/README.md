## Project Overview

Build and publish your own engaging Tic Tac Toe experience without starting from scratch. This solution combines the timeless gameplay of Tic Tac Toe with modern multiplayer technology, player progression, and built-in monetization. Whether players want to challenge an AI opponent, compete with friends on the same device, or battle real players online, everything is already included.

Powered by Flutter and Firebase, the game offers fast real-time matchmaking, multiple board sizes, a virtual coin economy, cosmetic skins, global leaderboards, multilingual support, and seamless authentication. Every component is designed to help you launch quickly while giving players a polished, enjoyable gaming experience.

Simply customize the branding, connect your Firebase project, publish to Google Play and the App Store, and start growing your own gaming community.

## Why Choose This Product

- **Real-Time Online Multiplayer** — players are automatically matched with opponents worldwide using Firebase Realtime Database for fast, responsive gameplay.
- **Multiple Ways to Play** — AI opponent, local same-device friends, or real players online.
- **Built-In Coin Economy** — virtual coins create exciting competitive matches and encourage long-term engagement.
- **Flexible Game Boards** — 3×3, 4×4, and 5×5 boards for beginners and experienced players alike.
- **Ready-to-Earn Monetization** — Google Play & Apple In-App Purchases, AdMob, Unity Ads, and permanent ad removal.
- **Player Progression** — leaderboards, match history, rankings, skins, and statistics.
- **Quick & Secure Authentication** — Google, Apple, Email, and Guest Login, with guest-to-permanent account upgrade.
- **Highly Customizable** — rebrand with your own logo, colors, assets, Firebase project, and store listings.
