# Core Features Deep-Dive

## 01. Real-Time Online Multiplayer

**Compete against real players across the globe.**

<!-- TODO: replace with real online multiplayer / matchmaking screenshot -->
![Online multiplayer placeholder](images/placeholder.svg)

Move beyond traditional Tic Tac Toe with an exciting online multiplayer experience powered by Firebase Realtime Database. Players are automatically matched with opponents in real time, creating fast, competitive matches that feel smooth and responsive across Android and iOS. Whether someone is playing from the same city or another continent, every move is synchronized instantly, making each game fair, competitive, and engaging.

- Automatic opponent matchmaking
- Real-time gameplay synchronization
- Fast room creation & joining
- Stable Firebase-powered networking
- Cross-platform multiplayer
- Coin-based competitive matches

## 02. Three Ways to Play

**One game. Three completely different experiences.**

<!-- TODO: replace with real screenshot collage (online / AI / pass & play) -->
![Three ways to play placeholder](images/placeholder.svg)

- **Online Multiplayer** — compete against real players in ranked coin matches. Select your wager, find an opponent, and prove your skills to earn rewards and climb the leaderboard.
- **Practice with Dora** — challenge Dora, the built-in AI opponent, to improve your strategy before entering competitive matches. Multiple difficulty levels and board sizes let players sharpen decision-making without risking coins.
- **Pass & Play** — play locally with friends and family on a single device. Perfect for classrooms, road trips, gatherings, or anyone wanting to play offline.

## 03. Beyond the Classic 3×3 Board

**Choose the challenge that fits your style.**

<!-- TODO: replace with real 3x3 / 4x4 / 5x5 board size screenshot -->
![Board sizes placeholder](images/placeholder.svg)

Classic Tic Tac Toe is fun, but it can become predictable. This game introduces larger boards for deeper thinking, longer matches, and more strategic gameplay. Players can freely choose between **3×3, 4×4, and 5×5**. Larger boards create new winning possibilities and significantly increase replay value for experienced players.

## 04. Competitive Coin Economy

**Every match has something at stake.**

<!-- TODO: replace with real coin wager / entry screen screenshot -->
![Coin economy placeholder](images/placeholder.svg)

The integrated coin system transforms ordinary Tic Tac Toe into a competitive experience where players invest coins before every online match. Winners receive double the entry amount, while defeated players lose their wager, creating excitement in every game.

**Available entry levels:** 10 · 25 · 50 · 100 coins.
