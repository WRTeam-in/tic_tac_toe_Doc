# How to Change Music

1. Add your music file under the `assets/music` folder.

   ![Add music files to assets](../static/images/app/addMusicFilestoAssets.png)

2. Go to `lib/Helper/Constants.dart` and assign the name of the music file.

   ![Assign music in Constants](../static/images/app/changeMusicinConstatnt.png)
